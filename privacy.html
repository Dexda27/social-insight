Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Social Insight is a social media analytics application designed to help authorized users analyze social media content and engagement data.

2. Information We Access

When a user authorizes a supported social media platform, Social Insight may access information permitted by the platform and the permissions granted by the user, such as basic profile information and publicly available content or engagement data.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide analytics features, including content analysis, engagement analysis, customer insight, sentiment analysis, and trend visualization.

4. Data Storage

Social Insight stores only data necessary for application functionality. Social media passwords are not collected by Social Insight.

5. Data Sharing

Social Insight does not sell personal information. Data is not shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the application, comply with applicable law, or as required by connected platform services.

6. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect application data. Users should keep credentials and access tokens confidential.

7. User Control

Users may revoke application access through the relevant social media platform's account settings.

8. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner through the contact information associated with this project.
